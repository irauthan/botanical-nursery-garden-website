In Honor of Mr. Virendra Singh Gusain

Founder, Visionary & Nature Guardian (Estd. 1985)

"A tree planted with love outlives generations. When we tend to the earth with care, nature repays us with boundless shade, sweet fruits, pure oxygen, and tranquility."

A Living Legacy of Four Decades

The story of Virendra Garden started in 1985 when Mr. Virendra Singh Gusain, deeply inspired by the natural beauty of Uttarakhand, envisioned transforming fertile soil into an organic botanical heaven.

Starting with native mango varieties, he painstakingly grew over 100+ grand Mango trees, followed by Himalayan Walnut (Akhrot), Peach (Aadu), Litchi, Banana, Guava, Pomegranate, Pear (Nashpati), Jamun, and fragrant Lemon Grass.

Today, after 40+ years, Virendra Garden stands not only as an orchard but as an ecological testament to how one man’s love for trees can create a sanctuary that breathes life into the entire region.

Chronicle of Growth

Historical Milestones (1985 – 2025+)

1985 — The Foundation & First Mango Saplings

Mr. Virendra Singh Gusain established the garden by planting the first batch of native mango saplings with organic soil conditioning.

1992 — Walnut, Peach & Pear Expansion

Introduction of 10+ Walnut (Akhrot) trees, 10+ Peach (Aadu) trees, and crisp Pear (Nashpati) varieties suited to mountain weather.

2002 — Litchi, Banana & Pomegranate Orchards

Expansion reached over 100 mango trees along with succulent litchi trees, banana groves, guava, and antioxidant-rich pomegranate shrubs.

2014 — Lemongrass & Herbal Flora

Aromatic lemongrass, medicinal herbs, and perennial flowers were integrated along walking borders to support honeybees and butterflies.

2020 to Present — 40+ Years of Eco-Tourism & Tree Adoption

Welcoming visitors, school botanical tours, pre-wedding photography, and empowering guests to sponsor and plant trees for nature.
