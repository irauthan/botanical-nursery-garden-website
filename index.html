Seasonal Experience Guide

What’s Blooming & Fruiting Right Now?

Every season brings a fresh visual delight and natural harvest in our mountain orchards.

Blossom & Fragrance Season

Peach Blossoms & Mango Floral Canopy

Spring transforms Virendra Garden into a pink and golden wonderland. Peach trees (आड़ू) burst into enchanting pink flowers while 100+ mango trees bloom with sweet aromatic baur (बौर), attracting hundreds of pollinators.

  • 🌸 Pink mountain peach & pear flower blossoms
  • 🐝 Active honeybee pollination & pleasant 20°C weather
  • 📸 Best season for vibrant pre-wedding & couple photography
Harvest & Organic Feast

100+ Mangoes, Litchi & Juicy Peaches

Summer is our sweetest season! The branches of our 40-year-old trees bow with juicy, naturally ripened chemical-free mangoes, succulent litchis, and fresh peaches. Shaded canopies keep the garden 5°C cooler than the city.

  • 🥭 Fresh organic mango picking & tasting experience
  • 🍒 Juicy red Litchi bunches ready for harvest
  • 🧺 Shaded lawn picnics under grand 40-year mango tree canopies
Nut Harvest & Golden Sunsets

Himalayan Walnut (Akhrot) & Mountain Pears

Autumn brings clear Himalayan blue skies and the harvest of our sturdy Walnut trees. The garden trails are draped in golden afternoon sunlight, making evening walks therapeutic and unforgettable.

  • 🌰 Organic Walnut (Akhrot) collection & curing
  • 🍐 Crisp mountain pears (Nashpati) & Pomegranates
  • 🌄 Unobstructed crystal clear sunset horizon
Cozy Sunshine & Herbal Tea

Lemongrass Aroma, Birdwatching & Warm Sun

Winter at Virendra Garden is a peaceful retreat. Soak in gentle mountain sunshine on open lawns, sip fresh brewed herbal tea from our Lemongrass beds, and spot migratory Himalayan birds.

  • 🍵 Freshly steeped organic Lemongrass mountain tea
  • 🐦 Prime birdwatching season for mountain songbirds
  • ☀️ Warm winter sunbathing on manicured stone-paved trails
